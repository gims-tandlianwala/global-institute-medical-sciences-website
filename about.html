About the institute

Tandlianwala's only registered medical & computer college

Council-approved diplomas, hospital-based training, and practical computer courses — all under one roof, led by a director committed to this town's students.

Director's message

"Education and hands-on hospital training, together — that is how we prepare our students for a real career in health care and technology."

Mian Muhammad Waqas
Director, Global Institute of Medical Sciences & Computer College

Mian Muhammad Waqas founded Global Institute of Medical Sciences & Computer College to give the students of Tandlianwala access to council-approved medical, nursing and computer education without having to travel to a big city. Under his leadership the institute has built a track record of strong exam results, hands-on hospital training partnerships, and affordable fee packages — including free coaching classes for matric-passed and result-awaiting students.

For any question about admissions, fee packages or programs, you can reach the director's office directly at any time Mon-to-Sat on 9:00 A.M to 5:00 P.M and for more information Contact on these Numbers 0300-6651431 or on WhatsApp at 0312-6651341.

What drives us

Mission & vision

Our Mission

To provide affordable, council-approved medical, nursing and computer education to the students of Tandlianwala and nearby areas — backed by qualified doctors, experienced paramedical staff, and hospital-based practical training.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted name in health-sciences and IT education, sending well-trained nurses, midwives, technicians and computer professionals into hospitals, clinics and workplaces across Punjab.

Approved & registered

Our approvals, in detail

Tandlianwala's only registered institute — every medical program is approved by a recognised council.

Approves our CNA, CMW and LHV nursing & midwifery programs.

Punjab Pharmacy Council, Lahore

F. No. 2-176/2023-PCP

Approves our Pharmacy Technician (Category-B) and Dispenser diplomas.

Punjab Medical Faculty, Lahore

Reg. No. 174388-PMF

Approves our RIT, MLT, OTT, DISP and LHV diplomas (F.Sc-equivalent).
