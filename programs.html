LHV

Lady Health Visitor

2 Years · Pakistan Nursing & Midwifery Council · PNMC F-11-INST-2025/16-214(a)
+
Eligibility Criteria
  • Matric (Science / Bio Science) with 45% marks
  • Age limit: 35 years
Required Documents
  • Matric Result Card & Certificate
  • Applicant CNIC / B-Form copy
  • Father / Guardian CNIC copy
  • Domicile Certificate
  • 12 passport size pictures
  • White dupatta or white scarf (mandatory)
What You'll Train In
  • Community health care
  • Women & child health
  • Hospital & field practical training
Career Path
  • Community Health Inspector (CHI) — government jobs

  • Own community health practice
